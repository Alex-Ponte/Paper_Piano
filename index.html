Tips
  • Play: tap a key firmly enough to make a small knock – the mic hears when, the camera sees where.
  • Wrong key? Raise the sync delay in Settings, or choose “Index fingers only”.
  • Taps missed? Raise sensitivity (the meter flashes orange when a tap is heard). Random notes? Lower it.
  • Phone: turn on rotation lock, keep the phone and paper still, and turn the volume up (iPhone: check the silent switch). Wired earphones + “Headphones” give longer notes.
